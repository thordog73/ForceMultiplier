# Force Multiplier 22.9.4 — Documentation downtime recovery

- Preserves the entire 22.9.3 offline app, existing 24-aircraft free-form modification sandbox and 90-day scheduling capstone.
- Adds **Recover Flying Days** to the Documentation sequence between Modification Plan and Inspection Flow.
- Learner sees a flawed, two-cure schedule and an editable copy; can erase, rearrange, reset, clear, check, reveal the model, or advance to another flawed case.
- Displays weeks with Saturday and Sunday shaded as non-work days. Validated final cure must last the assumed full 24-hour block; it may elapse over a weekend.
- Gives actionable correction feedback: two cures, wrong order, weekend active work, gaps, cure timing, wasted weekday cure, or late fleet return.
- Computes flying weekdays unavailable and earliest ready-to-fly weekday. Illustrative model saves three or four flying weekdays depending on the starting case.
- Makes the combined cure strictly scenario-authorized; does not recommend bypassing required real-world cure or QA procedures.
- Adds new automated route, model, user-interaction and edge-case tests.
