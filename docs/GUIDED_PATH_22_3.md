# Force Multiplier 22.3 graduation path

## Training designations
- **4 hours — Graduate:** approximately 40 minutes in each of the five functions (200 minutes total), then a 40-minute integrated graduation challenge. All 100 fundamentals are encountered and applied.
- **12 hours — Advanced Graduate:** three independent successful cases per fundamental, transfer across at least two worlds, reduced cueing, and an advanced integrated challenge.
- **24 hours — Expert:** five independent successful cases per fundamental, transfer across at least three worlds, routine automation eligibility, and an expert cross-function supervisor challenge.

These are Force Multiplier product designations. They are not official Air Force skill levels, occupational certification, workplace authorization, or AMMOS equivalency.

## Progressive difficulty
Teach → Recognize → Integrate → Transfer → Supervise. Later hours should use fresh conditions, different worlds, conflicting evidence, disruptions, and cross-function cases rather than identical repetitions.

## Four-hour budget
Each function contains eight guided packets at about five minutes each = 40 minutes per function. Five functions = 200 minutes. The integrated graduation challenge is budgeted at 40 minutes, for a 240-minute core.
