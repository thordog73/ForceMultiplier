# Force Multiplier 22.9.1

## Analysis
- Difference walkthroughs now calculate Welch t or Mann–Whitney U from fictional raw rows and show the actual statistic/p-value.
- Correlation walkthroughs calculate Pearson r, t, df, and p-value.
- Big Boss monthly verification now cycles across six hidden outcomes: genuine improvement, no change, worse, delayed improvement, false-positive risk, and false-negative risk.
- End-of-cycle decision asks whether to standardize, reopen the investigation, or verify the source data.

## Documentation
- Inspection Flow is now learner-built from due dates.
- Phase Flow is now learner-built from hours remaining and planned weekly utilization.
- Both support Clear, Show Model, Check, and Show Me behavior.

## Assessment quality
- Multiple-choice wording was rewritten so correct-answer length is not a reliable cue.
- Automated answer-length audit currently finds the correct answer is longest in only 2 of 14 audited questions.
