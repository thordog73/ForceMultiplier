# Force Multiplier 22.8.2 — Documentation 24-Aircraft Monthly Modification Flow

- Replaces the four-aircraft Documentation opening ordering exercise with a 24-aircraft monthly rollout board.
- Supplied sequence begins A/C 001 Day 1, A/C 021 Day 2, A/C 014 Day 3, A/C 033 Day 4, then continues one new aircraft per day through Day 24.
- Every aircraft automatically displays RED work Day 1–2, GREEN work Day 3–4, and one final 24-hour cure Day 5 relative to its start.
- The final aircraft clears the pipeline by Day 28, making the modification flow visible across the full month.
- Adds a Day 1–28 preview slider with RED/GREEN/CURE/COMPLETE/NOT STARTED fleet counts.
- Start days remain fully editable after wrong checks; no answer lock was added.
- Retains the next-step Documentation emphasis on controlling requirement, applicability, evidence, approvals, status and closeout.
- Scheduling 100-repetition and progressive 400-hour Phase Flow changes from 22.8.1 are retained.
