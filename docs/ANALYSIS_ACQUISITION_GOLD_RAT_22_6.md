# Force Multiplier 22.6.1 — Analysis Acquisition + Gold Rat Game

Analysis now uses one continuous organization case instead of 20 disconnected questions.

## Part 1 — Build the measurement system

The learner receives money to buy one of two organizations. The first case is an airline. A full case stays fixed across all 20 metric levels. Each metric level uses the same interaction pattern:

1. Read the business question and raw facts for both companies.
2. Choose the numerator.
3. Choose the denominator.
4. Choose percent / per-100 / ratio display.
5. Calculate both companies.
6. Add the completed metric to the acquisition dashboard.

The 20 metrics are: MC, historical Aircraft Availability, NMCM-only, NMCS-only, NMCB, TNMCM, TNMCS, Break, 8-hour Fix, Repeat, Recur, combined Repeat/Recur, CANN, Ground Abort, Air Abort, Scheduling Effectiveness, historical Maintenance Scheduling Effectiveness, Deferred Discrepancies per asset, Average mission duration, and Utilization.

Commander-style prompts from the earlier Analysis trainer are retained beside the metric builder, especially the denominator and TNMCM/TNMCS/NMCB challenges.

After all 20 metrics are built, the learner chooses which company is healthier using the dashboard rather than raw volume.

## Part 2 — Gold Rat Lab

The learner takes control of the weaker company. The laboratory is an eight-step improvement game:

1. Snitch on the business: identify the biggest actionable metric gap.
2. Trend: determine whether the problem is changing over time.
3. Difference: isolate the group that performs differently.
4. Correlation: identify an association without claiming causation.
5. Fishbone: turn clues into a testable explanation.
6. Identify the system constraint.
7. Choose a focused intervention that attacks the constraint.
8. Verify whether the intervention improved the target and the larger system.

The final payoff is the Gold Rat result: the learner did not merely report that the company was worse; they found the limiting problem, changed it, and verified improvement.

## Reset behavior

`New organization / reset the 20-metric workup` clears the current Analysis level progression, advances the case seed, and cycles the scenario world through airline, bus company, delivery fleet, and rail operator while preserving lifetime hidden-skill coverage.
