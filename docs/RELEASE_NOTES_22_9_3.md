# Force Multiplier 22.9.3

## Scheduling Capstone

- Adds an eighth Scheduling experience: **90-Day Fleet Scheduling Capstone**.
- Learner inherits a 24-aircraft fleet.
- Daily requirement: 12 aircraft in the first departure wave and 10 turns.
- Dynamic Phase Flow responds to learner flying decisions.
- Aircraft can be NMC, in Phase, or generically UNAVAILABLE.
- Planned UNAVAILABLE reasons are intentionally not explained in Scheduling; Documentation provides that deeper maintenance-control context later.
- Tracks days since last flight to expose hangar-queen/utilization risk.
- Tracks daily and cumulative modeled flying-hour output.
- Allows Phase clustering and fleet shortfalls to occur as consequences rather than blocking the learner.
- Includes a 90-day campaign strip and final fleet-management score.
- Adds 90-day model-solution testing and ten synthetic strategy stress tests.
