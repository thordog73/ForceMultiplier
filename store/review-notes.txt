Force Multiplier is a complete offline educational puzzle/simulation app, provided directly by its creator, Fred Esser. It bundles the exercises and game logic in the installed iPhone/iPad app; it does not require a website connection to fetch its core content.

No account, demo credentials, purchase, subscription, special hardware, location access, or camera access is required. All five specialties and four worlds are available from the game menu.

Suggested review path:
1. Open the app. In Airline / Scheduling, tap six aircraft and launch the first wave, then tap four and launch the second wave.
2. Open Menu > Choose a specialty. Try Analysis, Documentation, Database Management, or Data Integrity. Each starts with a small interactive task.
3. Open Menu > Choose a level to inspect later challenges immediately. There is no required grind or purchase gate.
4. Open Menu > Choose a world to switch among Airline, Bus Company, Cat Hugs, and Acorn Ops. Worlds change the context of the same underlying curriculum.
5. In Scheduling, inspect later levels to place work on the checkerboard, account for cure time and resource constraints, then revise the plan after a disruption.
6. Use a hint or working example to inspect the feedback. A shown example is labeled separately from independently completed work.
7. Open Menu > Saved data to export or import a main-game backup, including stars and unfinished challenges. Reset all progress in that submenu clears both the game and practice workspace after confirmation.
8. Open Menu > Practice workspace for the longer exercises, statistics lab, local work products, and its separate backup/restore controls in Settings. Entries and imported files are processed locally. Export, copy, print, and sharing occur only when the user chooses them. Workspace exports show a notes/names notice before the user chooses a destination.
9. Open About, Privacy, or Help from the menu. These pages are bundled for offline access.

There are 55 main-game levels: 13 Scheduling, 12 Analysis, 10 Documentation, 10 Database Management, and 10 Data Integrity. Scenarios use generated fictional data and simplified rules. Scores are game feedback, not a professional credential. The app is independent and is not an official government or airline training product.

The app has no live AI service, chat feature, analytics SDK, advertising network, cloud learner account, or operational-database connection. Game saves and practice entries remain in the app's local storage, with native on-device backup. The system share sheet, document picker, clipboard, and print sheet are user-initiated.

Any separately published "100 perspectives" review is explicitly AI-simulated design feedback, not 100 human user tests or a claim of validated learning effectiveness.
