Start with one small challenge. Discover how a better decision changes the whole plan.

Force Multiplier turns practical workplace fundamentals into a game. Tap to choose resources, build a schedule, interpret a chart, assemble the right records, and repair a broken process. Each challenge adds one new idea, with short prompts and immediate feedback.

FIVE PATHS TO EXPLORE

Scheduling: Begin by selecting six aircraft, then choose four for the next wave. Work up to inspections, compatible modifications, waiting periods, competing commitments, and a checkerboard plan that must adapt when conditions change.

Analysis: Count what happened, choose a fair denominator, calculate rates, compare groups, explore averages and trends, and distinguish evidence from an unsupported conclusion.

Documentation: Match a requirement to the right resource, check the current revision, forecast what is due, arrange prerequisites, and verify the evidence of completion.

Database Management: Give the right access, limit its scope, retire expired access, match unique identities, repair dependencies, and work through a recovery challenge.

Data Integrity: Follow a value back to its source, handle unknowns and units, find duplicate events, reconcile conflicting records, and check a batch before reporting it.

CHOOSE YOUR WORLD

Airline, Bus Company, Cat Hugs, and Acorn Ops apply the same core skills to different vehicles and missions. Switch worlds or specialties whenever you want. Each keeps its own progress.

KEEP PLAYING

Force Multiplier is an independent educational game. Scenarios, records, and operating rules are fictional and simplified. Stars describe performance within the game; they do not certify professional competence or authorize real maintenance work.
