Common questions

How do I try another skill or world?

Open Menu → Choose a specialty or Choose a world. You can switch at any time. Each world and specialty keeps its own progress.

Use Choose a level to jump ahead or revisit a challenge. There is no purchase gate.

I'm stuck. What should I try?

Check the short goal and the visible constraints. Use a hint, undo a move, or restart the attempt. Menu → Show a working example shows a valid approach.

Working examples are practice; they do not count as independent completion. Choose New case for a fresh variation.

What do the stars mean?

Three stars indicate an independently solved case, two a corrected case, and one a case solved with a hint. A shown example is recorded separately.

Stars describe this game attempt. They are not a professional qualification.

Where is my progress saved?

The installed app saves completed levels, unfinished challenges, and settings on your device. The browser version saves them in that browser. There is no account or automatic sync between devices or between the website and the installed app.

Keep a backup before clearing data, changing devices, or reinstalling. The main game and the practice workspace use separate backups.

How do I make or restore a backup?

In the game, open Menu → Saved data → Export game backup and choose where to save the JSON file. Use Import game backup in the same submenu to restore it. This backup includes the main game's worlds, stars, settings, and unfinished challenges.

The practice workspace has its own Workspace backup and restore controls in Settings. Keep that separate backup to preserve its exercises, work products, notes, and reviewer entries. The installed app shows an export notice before you choose a sharing destination.

Importing replaces the corresponding local progress after confirmation. Choose a backup created by Force Multiplier and keep the original file. Files must be smaller than 8 MB.

How do I clear my saved work?

Choose Menu → Saved data → Reset all progress and confirm. This clears the game's saved progress and the practice workspace's local entries, including notes and reviewer names. Export both kinds of backup first if you want to keep that work.

To clear only the practice workspace, use its Settings → Reset this workspace control. Main-game progress stays as it is.

Resetting cannot delete copies you already exported, sent, printed, or saved in a device backup.

Can I play without internet?

Yes. The installed iPhone and iPad app includes the challenges, examples, and help pages. Core play does not require internet.

Opening the public website, sending email, accessing a cloud file provider, or sharing through another app may require a connection.

What if something doesn't work?

Try reopening the app. For a large checkerboard, rotate the device or scroll the board horizontally. If copying or sharing is unavailable, try exporting a backup to Files.

If you see a storage warning, export your progress before closing. Avoid resetting or reinstalling until you have saved the work you need.

For a bug report, include your device model, iOS or browser version, the world and level, and the steps that led to the issue. A screenshot can help.
