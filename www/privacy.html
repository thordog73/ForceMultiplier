Privacy

Updated November 11, 2026

Force Multiplier is an independent educational game created by Fred Esser.

The installed app works offline. Game progress and practice entries are stored locally. There is no learner account, advertising, analytics service, or automatic upload of your work.

You decide whether to export a file, copy or print work, share it through another app, or send a support email.

Information saved by the app

The app saves settings, selected worlds and specialties, completed levels, unfinished challenges, scores, and practice results so you can continue later. The practice workspace can also save explanations, work products, working-group notes, and reviewer names or comments you enter.

These entries are stored in the app's local storage, with an additional on-device copy used to preserve progress. In the browser edition they are stored in that browser. They are not sent to the developer by the game. There is no automatic synchronization between the installed app and the website.

Use fictional practice information. The app does not need real employee, customer, aircraft, or organizational records.

Exports, imports, sharing, and printing

When you choose an export or share action, the app creates the file you requested. A workspace backup or work product can include names, notes, and other text you entered. Review it before choosing a recipient or destination.

On iPhone and iPad, sharing uses the system share sheet. Importing reads the file you select in the system document picker. Copying places the selected text on the system clipboard, and printing sends the selected document to the printing service you choose. Other apps, file providers, recipients, or printers handle that information under their own practices.

The app does not send a copy of an export to the developer automatically. Device backups may include the app's saved data depending on your device and backup settings.

Retention and deletion

Saved work remains until you replace or clear it. To remove both the game's and practice workspace's saved entries, choose Menu → Saved data → Reset all progress and confirm. The main game and practice workspace have separate backup controls; export both backups first if you want to keep that work. The practice workspace's Settings → Reset this workspace control clears only its entries.

Clearing the app's data does not delete files already exported, shared copies, printouts, or copies in device backups. Manage those through the relevant app, recipient, storage provider, or device settings. Restoring a backup may restore its saved entries.

Support email

If you email the developer, your email address, message, and attachments are received through email services and used to respond to or investigate your request. This happens only when you choose to send a message. Do not send records or attachments you do not want to share.

You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the same address. Information needed for an unresolved request or applicable recordkeeping obligations may need to be retained.

The public website

The installed app includes its game and information pages. Reading those bundled pages does not require contacting the public website.

If you visit the public website in a browser, its hosting services receive technical information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as your IP address, requested page, browser information, and request time. Browser-based progress is stored locally in your browser. The game does not add advertising or analytics trackers.

Apple and other services

Apple may process information when you download an app, use the App Store, enable device backups, or choose to share diagnostic information with Apple. Those services operate under Apple's policies and your settings. Likewise, email and sharing services operate under their own policies.

Force Multiplier does not integrate an advertising network, cross-app tracking, a live AI service, or a remote learner database. Changes that add data collection will be reflected in this policy and the applicable store disclosures.

Questions or privacy requests

Contact Fred Esser at fredesser@yahoo.com. For help with backups or resetting progress, visit Help & support.
